# Castle Capture

A medieval real-time strategy game that runs in the browser. Drag between castles to march tiny soldiers, claim neutral keeps, and conquer rival kingdoms.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. There's no build step and nothing to install.

## How to play

- **Drag** from one of your blue-banner castles to any castle to send troops.
- **Keys 1–4** or the **scroll wheel** choose how many to send: 25%, 50%, 75% or all.
- **Drag across** several of your castles to attack from all of them, or **tap** castles to select them and then tap a target.
- **Armies that meet on the road fight.** The larger column marches on with what's left.
- **Red banners** over your castles count the enemy troops marching on them. A pulsing ring means the castle will fall unless you reinforce it.
- **Bigger castles** train troops faster. Grey, unclaimed keeps never grow.
- **Space** selects all your castles. **P** pauses. **M** mutes the sound.

## Modes

- **Skirmish:** A random map against 1 or 2 AI kingdoms at Squire, Knight or Warlord difficulty.
- **Campaign:** Eight battles, from Thornbury to the High Throne. Each win unlocks the next.
