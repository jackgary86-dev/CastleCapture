Campaign

Eight battles across the rival homelands. Your chosen army fights them all.

Rules of war
  • Drag from your castle to any castle to send troops. Keys 1–4 or the scroll wheel choose how many: 25%, 50%, 75% or all.
  • Drag across several of your castles to attack from all of them. Or tap castles to select, then tap a target.
  • Special power: press Q or click the power panel in the top left of the map. The panel shows what your power does and counts down until it's ready: 45 seconds into the battle, then every five minutes.
  • Coins: every castle you hold earns coins each minute: small 1, medium 2, large 3. Save up to buy one map unit per battle (B opens the shop): a Ballista Tower (40), Siege Trebuchet (65) or Great Ward (90), placed near your castles.
  • Armies that meet on the road fight. The stronger column marches on with what's left.
  • Red banners over your castles count the enemy troops marching on them. A pulsing ring means the castle will fall unless you reinforce it.
  • Rivals surrender when they're down to a tenth of the troops on the map and two castles or fewer. Against one rival, their castles open their gates to you.
  • Rally points: right-drag (or Shift-drag, or long-press then drag on a touchscreen) from one of your castles to another. New troops then march there automatically, leaving 5 at home. Right-click the castle, or drag and release on empty ground, to clear it.
  • Saving: an unfinished battle is saved in this browser when you close or hide the tab, and every 10 seconds. Resume it from the top of this menu. Retreating to the menu abandons it.
  • Upkeep: a castle trains at half speed once its garrison passes twice its size, and a quarter speed past four times. An hourglass on its plaque shows when. Spread big armies out or put them to work.
  • Bigger castles train troops faster. Unclaimed keeps never grow. A captured castle is rebuilt in its new owner's style.
  • Space selects all your castles. [ and ] change the game speed (1×, 1.5× or 2×). P pauses. M mutes sound effects and N the music.
