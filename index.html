Rules of war
  • Drag from your castle to any castle to send troops. Keys 1–4 or the scroll wheel choose how many: 25%, 50%, 75% or all.
  • Drag across several of your castles to attack from all of them. Or tap castles to select, then tap a target.
  • Armies that meet on the road fight. The larger column marches on with what's left, so you can intercept attacks.
  • Red banners over your castles count the enemy troops marching on them. A pulsing ring means the castle will fall unless you reinforce it.
  • Bigger castles train troops faster. Grey, unclaimed keeps never grow.
  • Space selects all your castles. P pauses. M mutes.
